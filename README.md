# 🛒 Supermarket Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard built on a supermarket sales dataset (1,000 transactions, 17 columns), covering Power Query cleaning, data modeling, DAX measures, KPIs, and business insights across 4 report pages, plus drill-through and tooltip views.

## 🧰 Tools & Techniques Used

- Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)
- Data Modeling (Date table, relationships)
- DAX (calculated measures, time intelligence, ranking)
- KPI Cards, Slicers, Drill-through, Tooltips, Bookmarks
- Data Visualization & Business Insights

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Giza** was the top-performing branch with ₹110,568.71 in sales, followed by Alex and Cairo.
- **Food and Beverages** was the best-selling product line (₹56,144.84).
- **Member customers** generated ₹189,694.76 in sales vs. ₹133,271.99 from Normal customers.
- **Female customers** contributed more sales (₹194,671.84) than male customers (₹128,294.91).
- **Cash** was the most-used payment method by sales value (₹112,206.57), closely followed by Ewallet (₹109,993.11).
- Overall average customer rating: **6.97 / 10**.
